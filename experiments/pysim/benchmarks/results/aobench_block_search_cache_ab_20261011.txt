The three-process medians do not show a stable speed difference. Trial 1 was about twice as slow for both variants and both engines; the reported median-of-three is determined by trials 2 and 3. An additional sequential Interpreter pair measured 429.885 ms with cache (`425.777,426.409,428.595,431.739,433.296,429.885,431.671`) and 442.336 ms without cache (`442.336,440.124,451.669,443.053,437.222,437.678,462.534`), but its order was not reversed and the interleaved pairs did not reproduce that difference. Treat the AO-Bench timing evidence as inconclusive; retain the requested cache contract.
